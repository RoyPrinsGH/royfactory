How it works

No showroom, no sales visit, no pressure. Three steps from first email to finished room.

1 · Survey

We measure the room properly, check the walls and floors for square, and talk through what you need to store.

2 · Drawings

You get elevations and a fixed written quote. Revisions are free until you're happy with it.

3 · Build & fit

Built in the workshop, then fitted in two to four days with dust sheets and a clean site each evening.
